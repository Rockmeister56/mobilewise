❓ What's the difference between fixed-rate and adjustable-rate mortgages?
Fixed-rate mortgages lock in your interest rate for the entire loan term (typically 15-30 years), providing payment stability. Adjustable-rate mortgages (ARMs) start with a lower rate for an initial period (3-10 years), then adjust periodically based on market indexes. ARMs are great if you plan to sell or refinance within the fixed period.

❓ Should I choose a 15-year or 30-year mortgage?
30-year mortgages offer lower monthly payments but higher total interest. 15-year mortgages have higher payments but save significantly on interest and build equity faster. Our AI calculator can show you exactly how much you'll save with each option.

✅ Qualification & Approval Criteria

❓ What credit score do I need to qualify for a mortgage?
Minimum scores vary by loan type:
  • Conventional Loans: 620+ (best rates at 740+)
  • FHA Loans: 580+ (500-579 with 10% down)
  • VA Loans: 620+ (lender dependent)
  • USDA Loans: 640+
credit-score minimum-requirements
❓ How much down payment do I need?
Down payment requirements:
  • Conventional: 3-20% (20% avoids PMI)
  • FHA: 3.5% (with 580+ score)
  • VA: 0% (for eligible veterans)
  • USDA: 0% (rural properties)

❓ How does debt-to-income ratio affect my approval?
Your debt-to-income (DTI) ratio compares monthly debt payments to gross income. Most lenders prefer:
  • Front-end DTI (housing only): 28% or less
  • Back-end DTI (all debts): 36% or less
  • Maximum acceptable: 43-50% with strong compensating factors

Absolutely! Self-employed borrowers need 2 years of tax returns showing consistent income. We use bank statement programs and non-QM loans for those with complex income situations. Our AI analyzes 12-24 months of bank statements to verify income.
