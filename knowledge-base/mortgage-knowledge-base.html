❓ What's the difference between fixed-rate and adjustable-rate mortgages?

Original expertise: Fixed-rate mortgages lock in your interest rate for the entire loan term (typically 15-30 years), providing payment stability. Adjustable-rate mortgages (ARMs) start with a lower rate for an initial period (3-10 years), then adjust periodically based on market indexes. ARMs are great if you plan to sell or refinance within the fixed period.

❓ How does debt-to-income ratio affect my approval?

Original expertise: Your debt-to-income (DTI) ratio compares monthly debt payments to gross income. Most lenders prefer:

  • Front-end DTI (housing only): 28% or less
  • Back-end DTI (all debts): 36% or less
  • Maximum acceptable: 43-50% with strong compensating factors
