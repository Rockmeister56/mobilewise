How to Test Your Client's Bridge

  1. Click "Generate Client Test" above
  2. Upload a screenshot of the client's website when prompted
  3. Two files will download: bridge-client.js and test-client.html
  4. Upload both files to Netlify (rename test file to index.html)
  5. Open the Netlify URL — you'll see their website screenshot with your splash screen overlaid
  6. Test all buttons: "Get AI help" and "Just Browsing"
  7. Send the Netlify link to your client for approval

Once approved, send the bridge file to your client for production installation.
